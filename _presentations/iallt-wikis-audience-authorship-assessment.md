title: 'Wikis in Language Learning: Audience, Authorship, and Assessment.'
abstract: This session looks at the role of audience, authorship, and assessment in
  two very different wiki projects – a semester long project in a Japanese course
  that linked students at a distance and a short term project in a single section
  of a French course. The presenters will discuss the flexibility of wikis as teaching
  and learning tools, demonstrating the range of audiences, authorship models, and
  assessment strategies teachers can consider when designing wiki projects. Participants
  will leave with new ideas for language learning wikis and a framework to use when
  planning and reviewing collaborative writing projects.
