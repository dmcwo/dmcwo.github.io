title: Moving Toward Student-Centered Learning with Collaborative Technologies
abstract: 'You’ve heard the buzz words: knowledge construction, collaborative group
  work, Web 2.0. But what does this look like in a real university course? How do
  instructors move towards student-centered learning with collaborative technologies?
  Presenters will share examples of courses that successfully use collaborative technologies
  to benefit student learning. Participants will learn how several tools are currently
  being used on-campus to facilitate collaborative learning and become more informed
  about pedagogical considerations for increasing student interaction through the
  use of collaborative technologies.'
