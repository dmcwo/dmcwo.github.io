title: 'Scenarios for Success: Building High-Impact Library Collaborations'
abstract: High-impact collaborations that put information literacy theory into practice
  across settings and disciplines help faculty address critical issues and learning
  needs. This poster illustrates possible project scenarios and features a cast of
  potential collaborators to show attendees how to connect with librarians to develop
  hands-on learning experiences. Through discovery, reflection, and application, librarians
  demonstrate the value of integrating information literacy skills to enrich students’
  engagement in their courses.
