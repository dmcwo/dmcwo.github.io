title: 'Collaborative Conundrum: What We Know About Group Work and Technology, but
  Often Forget'
abstract: Students and instructors have access to a broad range of collaborative technologies
  for group projects, but successful group work is not just about the technology.
  In this session, the presenters will use research data and personal experience collected
  from a yearlong program at the University of Wisconsin-Madison on “Technology-Enhanced
  Collaborative Group Work” to highlight the principles of successful group work.
  Attendees will participate in a discussion on preparing instructors and students
  for collaboration, designing appropriate group assignments, assessing the process
  and product of group work, and choosing the *right* technology for collaboration.
