👋 Hi there! Welcome to my portfolio and notebook. This is my place to share past projects, current work, and ongoing explorations of learning, libraries, knowledge building communities, organizational design, and the creative process.

## About me

I'm passionate about helping communities build knowledge. I strive to create sustainable services, co-construct inclusive pathways to academic success, and invent novel approaches to centering diverse learner voices in higher education.

I came to digital experience design through educational technology and ESL instruction, which has given me an unusual combination: a classroom teacher's instinct for listening deeply and a builder's desire to create human-centered systems. At UW-Madison, UCLA, and UC San Diego, I've done this by scaling-up DIY instructional platforms, founding learner-led design teams, and helping organizations re-envision their services and spaces through inclusive, participatory, and accessible design.

At the heart of my work are relational questions about the creative process: Who gets to lead? Whose voices are centered, and whose are missing? And what changes when the people closest to a problem lead the development of solutions?
