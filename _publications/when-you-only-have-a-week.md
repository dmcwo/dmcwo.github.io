abstract: The cascade of events following the global outbreak of COVID-19 produced
  exceptional examples of camaraderie, collaboration, and resourcefulness. In this
  article, we share two ways UCLA Library public services staff came together to rapidly
  re-envision learner-centered library services and support in response to the COVID-19
  campus shutdown. In both cases, library staff adapted existing services (e.g., workshop
  delivery and tutorial design) to embrace remote technologies and scale to meet the
  needs of a greater number of learners.
